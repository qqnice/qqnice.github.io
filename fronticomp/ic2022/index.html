IC 2022 | The 5th International Conference on Innovative Computing

Guam or Online | Jan. 20 2022

Home

The 5th edition was presented as a forum for researchers, engineers, scientists, and practitioners in information technology and computer science and engineering. The header gave Guam or Online | Jan. 20 2022; the body explained that, because of the pandemic, the format was online or on-site with concrete days to be decided in mid-January 2022, and was later clarified as online due to COVID-19.

Paper Submission

Full papers of 8–12 pages were requested, prepared with the Springer proceedings style files. Accepted papers were to appear in Lecture Notes in Electrical Engineering (Springer), with selected papers recommended for post-conference journal publication. Proposals for special sessions were welcomed on AI, soft computing, intelligent systems, perception, cognitive science, psychology, data analysis, and IoT.
