About

BICTA 2023 is a workshop on big-data, IoT and cloud computing technologies and applications, held in conjunction with the 6th International Conference on Innovative Computing (IC2023), in Singapore, Feb. 01-03, 2023. Its topics are big-data, IoT, cloud computing and services, and network and security. Its goal is to bring together academic, industry and practitioner participants to share ideas, problems and solutions.

Accepted papers are peer reviewed. Excellent and invited papers are recommended to an SCI-indexed journal, and accepted papers are recommended to SCI/SCIE/SSCI-indexed journals, with further instructions by email.

Both figures are reproduced here as shown; they are not reconciled.

The $650 figure is described as covering attendance at the workshop and co-located events for one person; extra participant registration applies if more than one author attends.

  • Currency base is USD, calculated at the real-time exchange rate.
  • An accepted paper requires "FULL registration" to be in the proceedings and to be considered for post-conference events.
  • Full registration includes access to all conference sessions, keynote lectures, lunches, coffee breaks, banquet and reception.
  • An additional processing fee may be requested if a paper is selected for a post-conference event.
  • Authors, or at least one author, are strongly recommended to register and present; non-payment makes a paper ineligible for publication.

Payment routes: bank wiring and online card payment (VISA, Mastercard, American Express).
