FutureIT 2024 was a workshop for researchers and industry practitioners working in information technology. Papers accepted for the workshop were to appear in its proceedings, with a later route to an extended journal version.

Submission Guidelines and Notes

  • Review was blind peer review, in English, through the workshop submission system.
  • Submissions had to be original and unpublished.
  • Length: at least 4 pages and no more than 6 pages, including figures, tables and references.
  • Accepted papers could be recommended to SCI, SCIE or SSCI journals, with further instructions sent by email.
  • Strong presented papers with more than 40% extension or revision could qualify for a journal recommendation.

Scopes and Topics

The topic list was stated not to be exhaustive:

  • Big Data and Cloud Computing
  • Embedded System and Robotics
  • Future Network and Mobile Communication
  • Signal Processing and IoT
  • IT Convergence and Platform
  • Artificial Intelligence and Ambient Intelligence

Important Date

No dates were shown under this heading in the capture. The only time reference shown was that a participant registration fee would be announced on the website after December 2023.

Registration

Fee for an accepted paper: USD 1,950. This covered workshop and co-located event attendance for one person; additional authors attending needed a separate participant registration. No registration deadline was shown.

Payment could be made by bank wiring or online registration. Bank details as captured: SUMITOMO MITSUI BANKING CORPORATION, Swift SMBCJPJT, SHIBUYA BRANCH, 20-2 UDAGAWACHO SHIBUYA-KU Tokyo, account 654-9512836, MR.N CO., LTD., 602 SAKURAGAOKACHO 29-31 SHIBUYA-KU TOKYO 150-0031 JAPAN, with the note FutureIT 2024 (PaperID). The payer was to put the paper ID on the form and send a photo or scan of the receipt to the Conference Manager; confirmation usually took 3–5 working days, after which an electronic receipt was issued for reimbursement. Online card brands: VISA, Mastercard, American Express. The exchange rate could be revised by the card company.
