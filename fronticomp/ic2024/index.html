January 23-26, 2024 | Providence University, Taichung, Taiwan

The 7th edition used the same general forum framing as earlier editions. It was held in Taichung, Taiwan, January 23–26, 2024, at Providence University, with special sessions on the same AI-related subjects. Its committee structure differs substantially from earlier years, adding Honorary, Steering, and Publication chairs, with heavily Providence-based lists.

Venue

Providence University, Taichung, Taiwan; address 4th floor, Providence Hall, 200 Sec. 7, Taiwan Boulevard, Shalu Dist., Taichung City 43301 Taiwan.
