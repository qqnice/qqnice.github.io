About

This was the 10th edition in the Frontier Computing series, held July 13-16, 2020, in Singapore, as an online event. Its introduction defines Frontier Computing more broadly — spanning cloud, ubiquitous, green, and service computing and related areas — and presents the conference as an open forum for current results across computer science and engineering. It lists earlier editions through FC2019 and FC_ABH (Taichung, Taiwan), and repeats the invitation to academic and industry researchers.

Unlike the 2021–2023 pages, no Springer LNEE publication paragraph, final-program section, proceedings section, photo section, or venue section appeared in the captured text for this page.
