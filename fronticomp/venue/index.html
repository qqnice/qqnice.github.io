FC2026 Asia Forum Venue

New Engineering Building, Dongguk University. Location: 4th Floor, New Engineering Building, Dongguk University. Rooms #4137, #4142, #4147.

Address: Dongguk University, 30, Pildong-ro 1-gil, Jung-gu, Seoul, 04620, Republic of Korea.

Transportation (Asia Forum)

  • Train: Airport Railroad Express to Seoul Station, then Subway Line #4 to Chungmuro Station.
  • Bus: Airport Limousine Bus #6001 to PJ Hotel (Chungmuro stn).
  • Taxi: approx. KRW 55,000 from Incheon International Airport to the venue.

Notices

Arriving via Chungmuro Station is recommended rather than Dongguk Univ. Station, because ongoing campus construction means walking from Dongguk Univ. Station may take approx. 30 minutes.
