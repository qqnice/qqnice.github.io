About the Journal

The journal is described as multidisciplinary, publishing original work on advanced computing approaches and forward-looking applications, and as a meeting point for high-tech technologies, intelligent systems, smart environments, context-aware experiences, and sustainable development.

Its stated aims are addressing social problems, improving environments through anticipatory or human-based computing, and examining impacts on individuals, groups, and society as a whole.

Accepted Article Forms

  • Theoretical papers
  • Literature reviews
  • Case studies
  • Software reviews
  • Research reports
