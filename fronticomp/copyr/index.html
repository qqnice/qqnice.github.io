Payment currency is USD. The note on the original page states that a JPY figure shown at checkout may move with exchange rates.

Notes

  • An accepted paper needs full registration for inclusion in the proceedings and for post-conference recommendation.
  • Full registration covers the publication fee and the attendance fee for the conference and related events.
  • The discounted rate requires an IEEE/IET membership number, marked on the bank-wiring receipt.

No deadline is shown on the original page.

Way to Pay

Two payment routes are given:

  • Bank wiring (details below).
  • Online registration by VISA, Mastercard, or American Express. Card-company exchange rates may possibly be revised.
