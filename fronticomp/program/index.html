Information For Paper Presenters

  • Papers are presented in parallel sessions of about 60-90 minutes, each with 4-5 papers.
  • Each paper gets 15 minutes of presentation + 5 minutes of Q&A.
  • Authors unable to attend on-site must submit a 15-minute presentation video, which will be made publicly available on YouTube through private links after the session (wording as shown on the original page).
