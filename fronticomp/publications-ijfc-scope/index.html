Primary Scope

The journal's primary scope falls into four groups:

(a) Cutting-edge computing theory and application

Including artificial intelligence, AI ethics and AI environmental impact, 5th-generation networks, blockchain, communication systems, cybersecurity, and nature-inspired systems.

(b) Frontier quantum computing in hardware and software

Including architectures, accelerators, algorithms and models, plus approximate, analog, inexact and probabilistic computing, neuromorphic, biologically-inspired and optical computing.

(c) Emerging computing theories, methods and applications

Including machine learning, deep learning and big-data analytics, soft computing, chaotic systems, novel architectures, internet of things, high-performance data analytics, data visualisation and interdisciplinary applications.

(d) Advanced design for human-in-machine settings

Including human and social computing across well-being, science, health, culture, government, education, knowledge, learning, commerce, business, tourism, social networks and social software.
