About

This was the 12th edition in the Frontier Computing series, held July 13-16, 2022, in Tokyo, Japan. Like the neighbouring editions, it served as a meeting place for academic and industry researchers in computer science and information technology, and it followed the series history through FC2021.

Accepted papers appeared in the Springer Lecture Notes in Electrical Engineering (LNEE) series, indexed by EI and SCOPUS. Selected high-quality presented papers, extended or revised by more than 40% (not the 35% threshold shown for FC2023), were recommended to top-ranked journals after the conference.
