About

This was the 11th edition in the Frontier Computing series, held July 13-17, 2021, in Seoul, Korea, as an online event. It followed the series history through FC2020 and used the same broad framing as the neighbouring editions, welcoming academic and industry researchers in computer science and information technology.

Accepted papers appeared in the Springer Lecture Notes in Electrical Engineering (LNEE) series, indexed by EI and SCOPUS. Selected high-quality presented papers, extended or revised by more than 40%, were recommended to top-ranked journals after the conference.
