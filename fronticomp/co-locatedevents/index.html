Policy for Affiliated Events

The conference may collaborate with affiliated events, workshops, symposia or other academically related activities run by independent organising committees. Those events run independently, with their own submission systems, technical program committees and peer-review procedures. Where appropriate, their committees may seek academic input from FC committee members to support coordination and proceedings quality.

Papers accepted by affiliated events may be considered for the official FC proceedings if they meet FC review, publication and editorial requirements; the final inclusion decision rests with the FC Organizing Committee after editorial review.
