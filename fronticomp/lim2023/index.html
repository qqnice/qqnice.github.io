LIM 2023 is a workshop held alongside IC2023, focused on language and literature information modelling. It is organised by the Dept. of Korean Language and Literature, Kyung Hee University.

Submission Guidelines and Notes

Draft papers of under 4 pages are invited for preliminary review. Accepted papers may be recommended to SCI, SCIE, or SSCI journals.

Registration

Fees are in USD, with real-time exchange applied. Full registration is required for inclusion in the proceedings and for post-conference consideration. It covers sessions, keynotes, lunches, coffee breaks, the banquet, and the reception. An extra processing fee may apply for post-conference selection, and failure to register risks non-publication.

Payment may be made by bank wiring or by online card (VISA, Mastercard, American Express).

  • Registration: USD 1,850
  • Full Registration: $1,850
