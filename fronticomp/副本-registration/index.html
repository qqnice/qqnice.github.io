Registration

The main fee table on the original was presented as images (PriceS.jpg, PriceB.jpg), so its numeric table could not be read from the fetched text. The text fees shown at the foot of the original were:

ItemFee

Notes

  • "++" means tax added.
  • All fees are in USD, and payment is in the same currency.
  • An accepted paper needs Full registration for proceedings and post-conference recommendation.
  • Full registration covers publication plus attendance at the conference and related events.
  • When a discount rate is used, the IEEE/IET membership number is required and is marked on the bank-wiring receipt.

Way to Pay

I. Bank-wiring

Payment by bank wiring, with the same bank details as the FutureIT 2024 page except that the note is FC 2023 (PaperID). The payer sends a photo or scan of the receipt to the Conference Manager; confirmation usually takes 3–5 working days, after which an electronic receipt is issued for reimbursement.

II. Online registration

Online card brands: VISA, Mastercard, American Express.
