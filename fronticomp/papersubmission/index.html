Submission Guide

Accepted papers go into the Springer LNEE proceedings, indexed by EI/SCOPUS. Review weighs originality, significance, technical soundness, presentation and clarity.

Post-conference extension

For FC2026, selected high-quality presented papers, with 30-40% extension and revision, are recommended to top-ranked journals. Note: this 30-40% figure differs from the >40% figure on the home, FC2025 and FC2024 pages in this batch; each page's figure is reported as captured and they have not been harmonised.

Presentation formats

  • Full-paper presentation: complete paper of 6-8 pages (maximum 10 pages).
  • Poster presentation: short paper of 4-6 pages.

The program committee may reassign an accepted full paper to a poster session where that fits its content or quality better.

Formatting and permissions

Formatting follows the Springer conference-proceedings style files (LaTeX and Word). The permissions paragraph directs authors to the rights-holder site or the Copyright Clearance Center, otherwise to a Permissions Request Form and/or Release Request Form for interviewees, identifiable individuals or property owners; where a rights holder supplies its own form, it must cover the required rights listed in the Springer third-party permissions guidelines.
