14th International Conference on Frontier Computing (FC 2024)

July 10 - 14, 2024 — Singapore.

The event brought together researchers and practitioners from academia and industry to share developments across computer science and information technology.

Preceeding [sic]

Accepted papers are in the Springer LNEE proceedings (EI/SCOPUS indexed), with the same extension model as FC2025: selected high-quality presented papers, with >40% extension and revision, recommended to top-rank journal. The official proceeding of FC2024 (published by Springer) can be downloaded at https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-981-96-2794-3.
