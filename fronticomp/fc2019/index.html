About

This was the 9th edition in the Frontier Computing series, held July 9-12, 2019, in Kitakyushu, Japan. Its introduction uses the broader definition of Frontier Computing seen on the FC2020 page — cloud, ubiquitous, green, and service computing and related paradigms — and the same open-forum framing, listing earlier editions through FC2018 and FC_ABH (Taichung, Taiwan).
