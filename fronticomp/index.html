16th International Conference on Frontier Computing (FC2026)

July 06-10, 2026 — Prague, Czech Republic and Seoul, Korea.

The conference ran as two parallel forums: an Asian Forum in Seoul and a European Forum in Prague. During the conference period, participants could join sessions across both forums, including on-site and hybrid sessions.

Publication and post-conference extension

Accepted papers are published in Lecture Notes in Electrical Engineering (LNEE) by Springer, indexed by EI/SCOPUS. For the post-conference extension model, selected papers with >40% extension are recommended to top-ranked journals.
