About

This is a distinct special-forum event, not the main FC2019 conference: the 2019 International Conference on Frontier Computing — Special Forum on AI & Blockchain in Healthcare, held January 19-21, 2019, in Taichung, Taiwan. Its introduction uses the same broad Frontier Computing definition and open-forum framing as FC2019 and FC2020, listing earlier editions through FC2018.

Its proceedings note differs from the main conferences: it points to The International Workshop on Future Technology, published by ISSN (as shown on the original page), rather than to a Springer LNEE volume.
