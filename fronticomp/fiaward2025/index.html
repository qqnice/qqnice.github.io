Fi Award Competition 2025

The contest is student-only and centred on recent multimedia-technology results. Teams submit complete software products covering the full development process. After evaluation by the Program Committee, finalist teams are invited to FC 2025 in Osaka, Japan to present, demonstrate, and receive awards at the conference.

PREVIOUS WINNERS of FI-AWARD

  • Multimedia Task — Game Content Generation of Super Mario Bros. Using Interactive Evolutionary Computation
  • Multimedia Task — Using Large Language Models as Non Player Character in Role Playing Games
  • Artificial Intelligence — Portfolio Optimization Using No-Regret Online Learning: A Case Study on US Stocks

TRACKS

Each team chooses one track:

  • Game Design — covering game development for mobile, PC and console, not limited to programming, using popular multimedia authoring tools.
  • Frontier Computing Applications in Artificial Intelligence or Interactive Technology — covering entertainment, education, museum exhibits and more; design is expected to show innovation or a standard patent application, and the end product is limited only by imagination.

Team size: 1 or 2 advisor(s), no more than 5 students.

FORMAT

Submit a video plus a PDF document. The video must not exceed 3 minutes and must accompany a PDF of 4-6 pages in LNEE format, via Word or Latex templates. The video goes to peer review with the document.

CONTEST RULES

Evaluation of Projects

The video and slide should show artefacts produced during development as requested in the project description. Each report is evaluated by at least 2 Program Committee members.

Judging

  • Originality of concept
  • Effective use of platform (OS and hardware)
  • Polish and appeal (ease of use and aesthetics)

Final Submission

Selected teams submit a final PDF based on review comments, 4-6 pages in LNEE format, and are invited to present and demonstrate at FC2025. Winners are selected during the conference by all-conference participants. Teams prepare all material needed to run and fully evaluate their applications. Each selected team is requested to register strictly — the page does not state a fee amount here.

Copyright Issues

Unless exceptions are stated in advance, team artefacts are treated confidentially by the Program Committee during evaluation, but a copyright release is requested from teams selected to submit a full report.

Fee note: No fee is shown on this page — no registration amount, currency, or payment route is given here, despite the "register strictly" line for selected teams. No fee has been invented.
