Venue — FC2026

FC2026 Asia Forum, New Engineering Building, Dongguk University, 4th floor, rooms #4137, #4142, and #4147.

Address: Dongguk University, 30 Pildong-ro 1-gil, Jung-gu, Seoul, 04620, Republic of Korea.

Transport

  • Airport Railroad Express to Seoul Station, then Subway Line #4 to Chungmuro Station.
  • Airport limousine bus #6001 to PJ Hotel (Chungmuro).
  • Taxi from Incheon: approximately KRW 55,000.

Please arrive via Chungmuro rather than Dongguk Univ. Station: campus construction means the walk from Dongguk Univ. Station is about 30 minutes.
