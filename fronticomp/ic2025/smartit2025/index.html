The International Workshop on Smart Information Technology

Home IC2025

The International Workshop on Smart Information Technology (SmartIT 2025) addressed researchers and industry in multidisciplinary information technology, including sensor-based technologies, cyber-physical systems, edge computing, big data analytics, machine learning, cognitive computing, cloud computing, and mobile/pervasive computing. Accepted papers were to appear in the conference proceedings, and selected high-quality presented papers with substantial extension were to be recommended to indexed journals.

Submission Guidelines and Notes

  • Blind peer review, in English, through the workshop submission system.
  • Work must be original and unpublished.
  • At least 4 and no longer than 6 pages, including figures, tables, and references.
