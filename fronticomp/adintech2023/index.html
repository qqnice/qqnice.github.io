ADINTECH 2023 is an advanced information-technology workshop. A line above its introduction on the original page refers to the FC2026 Main Conference; that line is kept as shown and is not corrected here.

Scopes and Topics

The workshop covers big data and cloud, embedded systems and robotics, future networks and mobile communications, signal processing and the Internet of Things, and IT convergence and platforms, including sensor topics.

Submission Guidelines and Notes

Review is blind peer review, in English. Papers must be at least 4 pages and no longer than 12 pages, including figures, tables, and references. Accepted papers appear in the proceedings, with a post-conference extension model: selected high-quality presented papers, extended or revised by over 40%, may be recommended to top-rank journals.

Registration

Fees are in USD, with real-time exchange applied. Full registration is required, covering sessions, keynotes, lunches, coffee breaks, the banquet, and the reception. An extra fee may apply for post-conference selection, and non-payment means no eligibility or publication.

Payment may be made by bank wiring or by online card (VISA, Mastercard, American Express). Bank details are the same as for LIM 2023, except the remittance note reads ADINTECH 2023 (PaperID).
