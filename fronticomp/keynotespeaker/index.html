Prof. Donghyun Kang

Associate Professor in the Dept. of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at Dongguk University, Seoul, South Korea.

His research sits at the intersection of system software, emerging storage technologies and advanced computing infrastructures, with pioneering work on operating systems, file and storage systems, NAND flash memory, Non-Volatile RAM (NVRAM) and system-level optimisations for Large Language Models (LLMs).

Contributions noted include next-generation storage systems, Zoned Namespaces (ZNS), programming schemes to mitigate I/O interference, and hardware-software co-design for high-performance, low-latency, energy-efficient systems for data-intensive workloads.

He holds a Ph.D. from Sungkyunkwan University and collaborates with global semiconductor and cloud storage leaders.
