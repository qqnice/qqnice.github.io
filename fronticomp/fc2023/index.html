About

This was the 13th edition in the Frontier Computing series, held in Tokyo, Japan, in July 2023 (see the inconsistency note above). It brought together researchers from academia and industry in computer science and information technology, covering both theoretical and practical work, and it followed earlier editions from FC 2010 through FC2022.

Accepted papers appeared in the Springer Lecture Notes in Electrical Engineering (LNEE) series, indexed by EI and SCOPUS. Selected high-quality presented papers, extended or revised by more than 35%, were recommended to top-ranked journals after the conference.
