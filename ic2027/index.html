This page gives the key facts for the 10th edition of the conference, held in Okinawa alongside Ubi-Media 2027. It also welcomes proposals for special sessions in areas including artificial intelligence, soft computing, intelligent systems, perception, cognitive science, psychology, data analysis, and the Internet of Things.

Submission Guidelines and Notes

  • Accepted papers are included in the Lecture Notes in Electrical Engineering (LNEE) series published by Springer, indexed by EI and SCOPUS.
  • Review is double-blind, assessing originality, significance, technical soundness, presentation, and clarity.
  • Selected high-quality presented papers, with 30–40% extension and substantial revision, may be recommended to top-ranked journals.
  • LENGTH: 4–8 pages, including figures, tables, and references.
  • LANGUAGE: English, including figures, tables, and captions.
  • Use the Springer conference-proceedings style files as templates.

Topics of Interests

Contributions may cover research results, projects, surveys, industrial experience, and creative ideas in the following areas, and are not limited to them:

  • Big Data and Internet of Things
  • Web and Internet Computing
  • Natural Language Processing
  • Operating Systems
  • Image Processing, Pattern Recognition
  • Embedded system and software
  • Game Theory
  • Intelligent Information & Database Systems
  • Modeling and Simulation
  • Cybersecurity and Information Assurance
  • Human–Computer Interaction (HCI)
  • Reinforcement Learning and Decision Systems
